🏛️ About Mars Paladin

One person, one game, and the people who helped along the way

❤️ It Started with a One-Yuan Board Game

As a kid I bought a Journey to the West board game at a corner shop for one yuan: a paper board, four pieces and one die. That board was lost long ago. This game started from that memory.

🎵 The Pipa Is Played by My Wife

My wife has been learning the pipa for two years. Every pipa part in the game is her own playing and recording.

🌏 Connecting Gamers Worldwide

Full multi-language accessibility and seamless multiplayer, bringing friends and strangers together across borders and languages.
